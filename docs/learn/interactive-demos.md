# Explore with sample data

See what WhiteBoxXAI shows an auditor or a board before you connect anything of your own. This takes
about 15 minutes and needs no code.

## 1. Load sample data

1. Sign in, or [create a free account](https://whiteboxxai.com/register).
2. Go to **Settings → Sample data & tour** and choose **Load sample data**.

Sample models, predictions, drift events, bias audits and reports are added to your account. The
header shows **Demo data — illustrative only** while they're loaded, so nobody mistakes them for your
own, and sample models don't count against your plan's
[Governed AI Systems](../get-started/governance-events.md#what-is-one-governed-ai-system). **Reset sample
data** on the same pane removes them.

## 2. Take the tour

Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K**) and choose **Take the product tour**. It stops at each section: Overview,
Needs Attention, Model Register, Assurance, Observability, Compliance & Evidence, Reviews & Decisions,
and search and shortcuts.

## 3. Read a dossier

1. Open **Model Register** (`g` then `r`) and click a sample system.
2. Read the four **Governance verdict** cards, then open **How was this computed?** on one of them. It
   names the records and the method behind the verdict.
3. Look through the **Signals** tabs: Drift, Fairness, Explainability, Lineage & versions, and the
   Model card.

Try switching **View as** between **Compliance Officer**, **Data Scientist** and **Executive**: the
columns and wording change, the evidence doesn't.

## 4. Check readiness for a framework

1. Go to **Compliance & Evidence → Frameworks** (`g` then `e`).
2. Pick a framework card and read its **N of M controls with approved evidence** and its open gaps.
3. **Open the framework** to see the detail: ISO/IEC 42001 controls by clause, EU AI Act risk
   classifications, or NIST AI RMF functions.

Readiness is always a count of evidenced controls, never a percentage. See
[Compliance frameworks](../account/ai-regulations.md) for why.

## 5. Present to a board

On **Overview**, choose **Present**. Step through *Where we stand*, *Compliance readiness*, *What
changed* and *Decisions* with the arrow keys, then **Print** to get one slide per page. Press **Exit**
when you're done.

## 6. Generate an evidence pack

1. Go to **Compliance & Evidence → Evidence packs**.
2. Choose **The whole organization**, PDF, and **Generate evidence pack**.
3. Open **Reports → History**, download it, and try **Share with an auditor** with your own email
   address to see the verification-code flow a recipient goes through.

## Next

When you're ready to use your own data, run the readiness setup (**Overview → Set up readiness**) and
follow [Getting Started](../get-started/getting-started.md), or work through
[From model to evidence pack](workshops.md).
