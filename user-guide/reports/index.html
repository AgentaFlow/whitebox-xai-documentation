    Reports¶

    You have a deadline: an EU AI Act milestone, an ISO/IEC 42001 audit, a customer's vendor questionnaire, a board meeting. You need a document you can hand over without translating it yourself first. The Report Studio produces it from the evidence WhiteBoxXAI already holds, in PDF, Word or HTML, with your branding, sign-off and a verified way to share it.

    It's under Compliance & Evidence → Reports, with three sub-tabs: Report types, History and Schedules.

    Why you can trust what's in a report¶

    • Every number traces to a computation. Explanations are live SHAP, LIME or DiCE output, and fairness and drift sections come from recorded audits and reports you can open by id.
    • Nothing is templated filler. A report type that lacks the data it needs says Needs data, with the reason, rather than generating a document with placeholder text.
    • Governance records are enforced, not asserted. Approvals, votes, control decisions and justifications in a report are the ones recorded in the platform, with who and when.
    • Every issued file can be checked later. Verify a file confirms that a document someone hands back is byte-for-byte the one WhiteBoxXAI generated.

    For how SHAP, LIME and fairness metrics are computed, see Assurance and the Feature reference.

    Report types¶

    Report type Scope What it contains
    Quarterly Board Pack Whole organization Trust, risk and compliance posture across every monitored model, in plain governance language, including a High-Risk AI Systems section
    ISO/IEC 42001 Statement of Applicability ISO/IEC 42001 Every control, your in/out-of-scope decision and justification, and a traceability matrix to the evidence for each in-scope control
    EU AI Act Annex IV Technical Documentation One AI system The Annex IV technical documentation for a high-risk system: intended purpose, design, data governance and risk management measures
    EU AI Act Conformity Checklist EU AI Act Your applicability decisions against the Act's conformity requirements, with justifications for anything out of scope
    GDPR Art. 35 DPIA / Art. 22 Automated-Decision Record GDPR Article 30 processing records and Article 22 automated-decision rights requests, in one accountability record
    CCPA ADMT Risk Assessment CCPA A risk assessment for automated decision-making technology under CCPA/CPRA
    NIST AI RMF Profile NIST AI RMF Your applicability decisions mapped to the framework's functions and categories
    Model Card One AI system Intended use, training data, performance and fairness evaluation results
    Fairness Audit One AI system The most recent bias audit: fairness scores, per-group comparisons and flagged severity
    Drift Report One AI system The most recent drift analysis, with the baseline and comparison periods it was measured against
    Evidence Pack Whole organization or one system Trust, risk and compliance evidence bundled for an auditor
    Auditor Traceability Matrix Whole organization Every in-scope control across every adopted framework, mapped to the evidence an auditor would check

    Status¶

    Each report type card shows whether it can be generated now:

    • Ready
    • Needs data, with the reason, such as "No bias audit has been run yet for credit_scoring", "No ISO/IEC 42001 applicability decisions are recorded yet", or "credit_scoring is classified limited risk; Annex IV technical documentation applies to high-risk systems"
    • Not available yet

    Generate a report¶

    1. Choose a report type, and the AI system if its scope is one system.
    2. Choose a format: PDF, Word (DOCX) or HTML.
    3. Click Generate. The report is saved to History.

    Customize… opens the template editor, and Saved templates lists your versions of that report type, with Edit and Generate.

    Templates and writing style¶

    The template editor has a live Preview. A template sets:

    • Template name, Format, Brand profile (or Organization default) and AI system
    • Writing style:
      • Audience: Compliance officer, Data scientist, Board and executives, Auditor or Administrator
      • Tone: Formal or Plain
      • Level of detail: Concise, Standard or Detailed
      • Spelling: US or UK
    • Glossary: replace a platform term with your organization's term (Platform says → Say instead)
    • Sections: include, remove and reorder sections, and Add introduction with placeholders
    • Default template: use this template whenever the report type is generated

    Save template keeps it, and Generate produces a report from it.

    Brand profiles¶

    Reports carry your organization's branding from a brand profile. You can keep several, with one set as the Default. Administrators edit them in Settings → Brand profiles; everyone else can view them. A profile sets:

    • Colours, with a contrast check
    • Font: Inter, Arial, Helvetica, Georgia or Times New Roman
    • Cover layout: Classic, Modern or Minimal
    • Header and footer text, Confidentiality marking and Disclaimer
    • Watermark: None, DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL
    • Page size: A4 or US Letter
    • Logo

    Margin text and the watermark repeat on every page of a PDF.

    History, sign-off and verification¶

    Report history lists every generated report, with Download.

    Sign-off¶

    A report can be signed off in three roles: Preparer, Reviewer and Approver. Use Sign as … on a report. Each person can hold only one role on a given file, so the person who prepared a report can't also approve it. Sign-offs are recorded against that exact file.

    Verify a file¶

    Verify a file checks a document you've been handed against the reports WhiteBoxXAI generated. If the file was altered after it was issued, it won't match.

    Share a report with an auditor¶

    Sharing a report with someone outside your organization, such as an external auditor or a customer's security team, doesn't mean emailing a PDF or creating them an account.

    1. In History, choose Share with an auditor on a report.

    2. Pick a preset:

      Preset Link expires after Watermark
      Share with auditor 30 days CONFIDENTIAL
      Quick preview 2 days DRAFT

    3. Enter the recipient's email address and Send share link.

    The recipient opens a page titled Evidence report shared with you, chooses Email me a verification code, enters the 6-digit code sent to that same address, and then Download report. The share list shows each share's state, such as Waiting for them to verify, Expired or revoked, and Revoke stops a share immediately.

    How the link is protected:

    • The link is a locator, not a credential. Opening it reveals nothing until the recipient proves they control the email address.
    • The download token issued after verification is scoped to that one report and expires after 15 minutes.
    • Revoking a share blocks any further code request or verification immediately.
    • Every step (created, code requested, verified, denied, viewed, revoked) is written to the audit trail.

    Schedules¶

    Schedules → New schedule runs a template automatically:

    • Name, Template to run and Active
    • How often: Every day, Every week, Every 30 days, Every 90 days, or Custom (a cron expression, in UTC)
    • Tell these addresses when it finishes: who is notified when a run completes
    • Also e-mail the report as a secure link → Send the report to: recipients who get the report itself, through the same verified share link as above, never as an attachment

    Each schedule shows Next run, Last run, Runs and Failures. Failed runs are retried, and each schedule runs once per period.

    From code¶

    Generate a report type through the SDK:

    types = client.reports.list()        # ids, names, scope and availability
result = client.reports.generate(report_type_id="fairness_audit", model_id=model_id, format="pdf")
html = client.reports.preview(report_type_id="fairness_audit", model_id=model_id)

    Report type ids: quarterly_board_pack, iso42001_statement_of_applicability, eu_ai_act_annex_iv_technical_documentation, eu_ai_act_conformity_checklist, gdpr_dpia_automated_decision_record, ccpa_admt_risk_assessment, nist_ai_rmf_profile, model_card, fairness_audit, drift_report, evidence_pack, auditor_traceability_matrix. Take ids from client.reports.list() rather than hard-coding them.

    A generated report is an export with an id. Download, share and sign it through the export endpoints:

    # Download
curl -L -o report.pdf https://api.whiteboxxai.com/api/v1/export/exports/{export_id}/download \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $WHITEBOX_API_KEY"

# Share with an auditor (presets: share_with_auditor, quick_preview)
curl -X POST https://api.whiteboxxai.com/api/v1/export/exports/{export_id}/share \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $WHITEBOX_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"recipient_email": "auditor@example.com", "preset": "share_with_auditor"}'

# Record a sign-off: "prepared", "reviewed" or "approved"
curl -X POST https://api.whiteboxxai.com/api/v1/export/exports/{export_id}/signoffs \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $WHITEBOX_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"role": "reviewed"}'

    Data exports in other formats (CSV, Excel, JSON, Markdown) for model performance, drift, bias, explainability, LLM monitoring, the risk register and the trust score are available from POST /api/v1/export/exports, with schedules under /api/v1/export/scheduled-reports. See the API Reference.

    Generating reports, sharing and scheduling don't count as Governance Events.

    FAQ¶

    Is a report a snapshot or live? A snapshot. A generated report records the evidence as it was when generated, so it stays stable for an audit. Generate again, or schedule it, to reflect newer data.

    What if part of a report has no data? It says so. An empty, labelled section is more defensible under audit than a filled-in one.

    Can an AI assistant generate reports? Yes, through the Python SDK. The MCP server has no report-generation tools. The compliance-assistant prompt tells it to give you the download link and date rather than paraphrase a report it hasn't read.
